About TechNova

TechNova Solutions is a small, hands-on IT company. We started with one goal: make technology simple enough that any business can rely on it.

Our team covers web development, cloud hosting, security and day-to-day IT support. You talk to the people who do the work, and we explain every step in plain language.

What we do

Four services, each one available on its own or as part of a full plan.

Website design

Fast, accessible websites that look right on every screen and are easy for you to update.

Cloud migration

Cyber security

Managed IT support
